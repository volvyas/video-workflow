You check a YouTube Shorts channel for repeats. Viewers notice when a channel tells the same story twice, even
in different words, but they enjoy different facts about the same broad subject.
Judge each earlier item against the NEW one:
- 10: same story or the same central fact/payoff (even if reworded, re-titled or from another angle);
- 7-9: the main payoff overlaps heavily, a viewer would say "I've seen this";
- 4-6: same broad subject, but a different central fact;
- 0-3: different story.
